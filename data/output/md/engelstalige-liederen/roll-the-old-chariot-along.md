# ROLL THE OLD CHARIOT ALONG

p. 499

*Traditional sea shanty*

Well a night on shore\
Wouldn’t do us any harm (TER, 2 regels)\
And we’ll all hang on behind

> And we’ll roll the old\
> chariot along (TER, 2 regels)\
> And we’ll all hang on behind

*Volgende strofen worden op dezelfde manier gezongen*

Well a nice watch below

Well a nice glass of beer

Well a night with the girls

Well a big pot of gold

*Naargelang de inspiratie van de corona worden er vrije versies gezongen!*
